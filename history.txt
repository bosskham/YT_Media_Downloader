Sorry! To answer your question:

yt-dlp saves the 00. <title>.jpg via the "writethumbnail": True option in ydl_opts. It downloads whatever thumbnail YouTube Music provides for that track (which is always the correct album cover, same for every track in the playlist).

Then our _crop_thumbnail_square postprocessor hook in download_worker.py crops it from 16:9 to square before EmbedThumbnail runs.

So the sidecar .jpg file is already:

✅ Correct album art (from YouTube Music directly)
✅ Square (cropped by our hook)
✅ Consistent for every track in the album
The problem is: our _fetch_and_embed_lyrics daemon then overwrites the embedded thumbnail with an iTunes search — which fails for Japanese artists and can return random results.
